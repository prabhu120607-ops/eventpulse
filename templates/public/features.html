Automation Engine

Automated Dispatch & Intelligent Reminders

Eliminate manual post-event email blasts. When a workshop or seminar concludes, EventPulse automatically checks verified attendance records, generates single-use cryptographic tokens, and emails personalized feedback invitations to eligible students.

  • Configurable dispatch triggers (immediate, +1h, +6h, +24h, or manual)
  • Automated reminder scheduler targets only non-respondents before deadline
  • Real-time tracking of sent invitations, delivery failures, and completion rates

Privacy Architecture

Verified Anonymity Without Data Distortion

Campus feedback requires honest critique. EventPulse offers two distinct feedback modes: Identified and Anonymous. In Anonymous mode, individual responses are decoupled at the database level from student IDs, while single-use tokens ensure each attendee can only submit once.

  • Zero association between student profiles and anonymous feedback comments
  • Cryptographic token consumption prevents duplicate submissions without identity linkage
  • Student confidence leads to constructive, candid reviews

Actionable Intelligence

Built-in NLP Theme & Sentiment Analysis

Organizers do not have time to read hundreds of unstructured feedback comments manually. EventPulse includes built-in NLP heuristics that categorize comments into positive praise, recurring complaints, and actionable suggestions.

  • Automated sentiment scoring across all submitted text
  • Keyword extraction groups themes (e.g. Venue, Audio, Practical Hands-on)
  • Generates executive event summaries ready for departmental review
