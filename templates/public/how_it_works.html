Step-by-Step Workflow

How EventPulse Works

Discover how EventPulse transforms chaotic post-event feedback into an automated, reliable campus process.

1
Step 1: Event Configuration

Organizer Creates the Event & Builds Form

The department coordinator or club organizer registers the event details, sets the scheduled end time, selects either Anonymous or Identified feedback mode, and customizes question types (star ratings, multiple choice, text questions).

Pre-set question templates for workshops, guest lectures, and hackathons are available instantly.
2
Step 2: Attendee Enrollment

Upload Registered Student Roster via CSV

Organizers import registered participants from registration forms or attendance scanners. EventPulse validates student emails, flags duplicate entries, and connects eligible students with the event.

Includes CSV column validation, preview mode, and import verification counts.
3
Step 3: Automated Dispatch

Automated Token & Email Delivery

Once the event concludes, the EventPulse scheduler triggers invitation creation. Each eligible student receives an invitation email containing a secure, personalized single-use feedback link.

Links look like: https://univ.edu/feedback/submit/vK9pX8... — no logins needed for students.
4
Step 4: Submission & Reminders

Frictionless Submission & Automated Follow-up

Students open the mobile-optimized form, submit ratings and suggestions in seconds. The single-use token is marked consumed. Students who haven't responded receive gentle automated reminders before the deadline closes the form.

5
Step 5: Insights & Accreditation

Analytics, Sentiment Themes, & CSV Reports

Administrators explore real-time satisfaction trends, department benchmarks, and AI theme summaries, and export CSV reports ready for curriculum committee meetings and institutional accreditation audits.
