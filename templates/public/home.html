Campus Operational Reality

The Challenge of Manual Feedback Collection

Traditional paper evaluation sheets and generic shared online links suffer from severe drop-offs, administrative overhead, and unrepresentative data.

Low Student Response Rates

Generic forms sent days late or announced verbally at the end of sessions result in response rates below 15%, missing crucial student perspectives.

Duplicate & Fraudulent Responses

Open forms cannot verify whether a respondent actually attended, leading to ballot-stuffing, spam, or duplicate responses that skew institutional metrics.

Unused, Dormant Reviews

Collected comments often sit dormant in spreadsheets without systematic sentiment clustering or automated executive reporting for university leadership.

Core Capabilities

Engineered for Academic Excellence

Every tool required to streamline feedback operations across faculties, guest lectures, and campus festivals.

Automated Invitation Trigger

Schedule feedback invitations immediately post-event or with customizable time delays. Automated reminders gently nudge pending respondents before deadlines.

Cryptographic Single-Use Tokens

Students receive unique URL-safe tokens that prevent duplicate submissions, ensure authenticated eligibility, and close automatically at the deadline.

Guaranteed Anonymous Mode

Support verified anonymous feedback where responses are decoupled from student IDs at the database level, fostering honest, transparent feedback.

Flexible Question Builder

Combine 5-star ratings, multiple choice, single choice, yes/no questions, and detailed constructive text inputs tailored for individual events.

NLP Sentiment & Theme Analysis

Built-in Python natural language processing automatically identifies positive praise, common complaints, and recurring student suggestions.

One-Click Executive CSV Exports

Export clean, formatted reports for individual events, departments, or university-wide satisfaction audits ready for academic compliance.
